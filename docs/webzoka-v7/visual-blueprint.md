# Webzoka V7 living visual blueprint

**Status:** Living blueprint
**Last updated:** 2026-09-10
**Surface:** Webzoka Public Web / default page `Beranda`

> **Decision precedence:** This document absorbs the historical V7 blueprint and the latest Chat decisions. The latest left-navigation decision explicitly replaces the historical recommendation for a top-navigation-only Public shell. Keep the V6-style left navigation; use the main canvas for richer editorial content and stronger visual hierarchy.

## 1. Positioning and messaging

### Primary positioning

> **Website untuk ditemukan. Sistem untuk operasional jalan.**

This is the hero-level promise. It explains Webzoka as both a credible public presence and an operational foundation without forcing a visitor to choose a product category first.

### Supporting message hierarchy

1. **Outcome:** A business becomes easier to find, easier to trust, and easier to run.
2. **Translation:** Website yang terlihat profesional. Sistem yang membuat kerja harian lebih rapi.
3. **Proof anchors:** `Mulai Rp600k` · `Launch 3–5 hari` for scope ready to review · renewal transparency.
4. **Pain statement:** `Berhenti Jadi Admin di Bisnis Kamu Sendiri.` This appears after the hero, never as the main hero headline.
5. **Human next step:** `Konsultasi WhatsApp`.

The homepage sells direction and confidence to start. It is not the Store catalog, calculator, project dashboard, billing area, or full product-demo directory.

## 2. Audience and voice

- **Primary audience:** Owner-led Indonesian SMBs that already have customers and revenue but still coordinate discovery, orders, booking, stock, reporting, or team work manually.
- **Good fit:** Rental and booking businesses, salons, clinics, shops, F&B, schools, and other small teams that use WhatsApp, books, and spreadsheets as their operating stack.
- **Voice:** Warm, practical, human, credible, and confident. Speak to owners as business partners, not as people who need to be rescued from technology.
- **Copy discipline:** Explain the next business decision in natural Indonesian. Use English only where it is already a clear product or technical term (`Website`, `Portal`, `Store`, `Hub`, `Live`, `Preview`).
- **Avoid:** Empty superlatives, enterprise jargon, “digital ecosystem” diagrams, patronizing “UMKM assistance” language, hype, and claims without attribution.

## 3. Product boundary and mental model

| Surface | Job | Content character | Handoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Public Web** | Establish trust and explain the business outcome. | Editorial, storytelling, rich, spacious. | Send scope/price/detail questions to Store; returning customers may use Hub. |
| **Store** | Help a buyer compare, inspect, price, and order. | Structured, searchable, commercial. | After purchase/trial, hand off to Hub when route is approved. |
| **Hub** | Let an existing customer manage the relationship. | Operational, dashboard-oriented. | Unified login for projects, systems, billing, support, and order tracking. |

Rules:

- Default Public destination is **Beranda**, not Store.
- Use one account label: **Masuk Hub**. Do not revive competing `Masuk Pelanggan` / `Masuk Layanan` labels.
- Hub route remains pending. Do not invent a URL or make a broken route look clickable.
- Public, Store, and Hub may share the same left-navigation family, but must not share the same content density or visual job.

## 4. Left-navigation shell

### Desktop

- Keep a persistent left sidebar as the V6 continuity point. Public must not become top-nav-only.
- Sidebar is a calm navigation rail, not a generic admin panel and not the page’s main visual language.
- Make **Beranda** the active/default entry.
- Group links by intent: Public navigation (`Solusi`, `Karya`, `Artikel`, `Harga`, `FAQ`), a distinct `Store` buying destination, and one quiet `Masuk Hub` account entry.
- Keep the consultation action available, but do not fill the rail with status badges or repeated CTA blocks.
- The content canvas should carry the premium feel through typography, asymmetric composition, real evidence, whitespace, and section rhythm.
- Public, Store, and Hub can share shell principles; their content canvas must remain respectively editorial, commercial, and operational.

### Sidebar character

- Warm paper or quiet light surface; deep ink text; restrained signal-blue active state.
- Premium and quiet: limited badges, no `NEW` / `HOT` / `PENDING` clutter, no decorative dashboard stats.
- Use spacing and type hierarchy before borders, color, or pills to show grouping.
- Keep Hub pending language factual and low prominence until route approval.

### Mobile

- Convert the sidebar into a drawer opened from a clearly labelled menu control.
- Menu control, drawer links, close control, and primary CTA must be at least `44 × 44px`.
- Close the drawer after navigation and keep keyboard focus/reading order understandable.
- Preserve the same mental order as desktop: Beranda → story → Store decision → Hub for existing customers.
- At `390px`, no horizontal page scroll. The drawer must not create a second page-wide overflow context.

## 5. Homepage anatomy and section order

Do not restructure this order in a polish round. Improve visual hierarchy and content depth within it.

### 0. Shell / navigation

Left-navigation shell with **Beranda** as the default Public destination. Expose Public anchors, Store, quiet Hub entry, and one primary WhatsApp action.

### 1. Hero — outcome before taxonomy

- Eyebrow: `Untuk bisnis yang ingin terlihat profesional dan jalan lebih rapi`.
- H1: `Website untuk ditemukan. Sistem untuk operasional jalan.`
- Supporting copy: Build trust in front and make daily work clearer behind the scenes.
- Proof anchors: starting price, qualified launch target, renewal transparency.
- Primary CTA: `Konsultasi WhatsApp`.
- Secondary link: `Lihat paket dan harga` → Store.
- Visual: one composed evidence frame, not a generic dashboard. Show a public website preview, a concise operational status card, and a restrained `ditemukan → dikelola` route.

### 2. Pain-to-outcome section

Use `Berhenti Jadi Admin di Bisnis Kamu Sendiri.` after the hero. Pair three lived problems with outcomes:

- `Sulit ditemukan` → a credible website that explains the business clearly.
- `Order dan status tercecer` → an operational flow with visible next steps.
- `Semua keputusan berhenti di owner` → a system the team can use consistently.

Keep this section qualitative and editorial. Do not use fear-heavy proof or add a standalone CTA here.

### 3. Website / Portal / Bundle decision

Explain the three buying models before detailed pricing. Use unequal, outcome-led cards rather than identical catalog tiles. Bundle can be visually featured, but it must not force an upsell.

### 4. Signature visual moment — front door → daily operations

Use one dark ink section to connect a public website preview to an operational frame. Keep the composition sparse and memorable; this is the only repeated Website + system connection the homepage needs.

### 5. Selected work / portfolio proof

Lead with one attributable featured project, then supporting previews. Every item gets an honest status and at least one traceable detail. Do not publish broken custom domains as live proof.

### 6. How it works

Use four steps: `Ceritakan kebutuhan` → `Pilih fondasi` → `Kami bangun dan rapikan` → `Review lalu launch`. Qualify the promise: `Target launch 3–5 hari untuk scope yang siap direview.`

### 7. Pricing and calculator handoff

Show `Mulai Rp600k` as a qualified starting price, separate initial scope from renewal/maintenance, explain what changes price, and hand off detailed calculation to Store.

### 8. Trust layer

Use attributable testimonials, approved customer logos, measurable case studies, and a short commitment strip. Until approval exists, show explicit placeholders or omit the element.

### 9. Practical FAQ

Answer Website vs Portal vs Bundle, starting-price scope, renewal, launch timing, adding a Portal later, live demo status, post-order flow, and project/support tracking. Link detail to Store or Hub only when the destination is confirmed.

### 10. Final consultation CTA and footer

Close with `Mulai dari kebutuhan yang paling penting.` and `Konsultasi WhatsApp`, plus a quieter Store link. Footer must keep Public Web, Store, and Hub boundaries visible without pretending Hub is ready.

## 6. Website / Portal / Bundle story

| Offer | Buyer need | What to explain | Buyer language |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Website** | Be found and trusted. | Public pages, positioning, inquiry path, portfolio, and content blocks. | `Saya butuh website` |
| **Portal** | Run work more consistently. | Operational flow, status visibility, team usage, and support path. | `Saya butuh sistem` |
| **Bundle** | Connect acquisition and operations. | Website + Portal with one joined starting path. | `Saya butuh keduanya` |

Required mental sequence:

```text
Website → Portal → Bundle
front door → daily work → connected system
```

At `390px`, preserve **Website → Portal → Bundle** in DOM and visual order. Bundle may remain recommended or visually emphasized, but must not appear first as an upsell. Store owns detailed comparison and calculator content.

## 7. Signature section rules

- Macro visual: `front door → daily operations`.
- Public frame: `Public Web` / `Preview` when it is a concept frame.
- Operational frame: `Live demo — Stock` only while the route is freshly validated.
- Connector: one clear route such as `ditemukan → dipahami → dikelola`.
- Content: no more than one operational status row, one visible action, and one proof annotation per frame.
- CTA: one action, normally `Lihat demo Stock` only when valid. Remove or demote duplicate actions.
- Dark section may be the one major paper → ink transition; do not add generic ecosystem diagrams, neon glow, charts without source, or repeated mockups elsewhere.

## 8. CTA hierarchy

- **Primary human action:** `Konsultasi WhatsApp`.
- **Pricing action:** `Hitung kebutuhanmu` → Store calculator.
- **Operational proof action:** `Lihat demo Stock` → only when route is confirmed live.
- **Secondary/context links:** `Lihat paket dan harga`, `Bandingkan paket`, `Lihat semua karya`, `Baca studi kasus`, `Masuk Hub`.
- One dominant filled CTA per major viewport or section. Avoid repeating a sales button when the section introduces no new decision.
- Mobile sticky CTA is optional. If used, show one WhatsApp action after the hero CTA leaves view, preserve safe-area space, and hide/reduce it near the final CTA.

## 9. Status vocabulary and honesty rules

Use a small readable label on every demo or project surface. Status meaning must be communicated by text and shape/icon, not color alone.

| Status | Meaning | Buyer-facing rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Live** | Route is working and safe to send to a visitor. | Link must be validated from the current build/review. Stock is the current candidate. |
| **Preview** | Visual or concept reference; not an active system route. | Use muted treatment and explicit copy such as `Preview only — not an active customer system`. |
| **Case study** | Story/outcome is available; live interaction may not be available. | Include business context and attribution; do not imply live access. |
| **Coming soon** | Useful roadmap item that cannot be mistaken for current proof. | Use sparingly, never as a substitute for route validation. |

Never label LMS, Clinic, Pharmacy, Travel/Rental, or Laundry demos `Live` until each route is validated. Never use broken custom portfolio domains as live proof. Do not use `bukan mockup` repetition as a trust substitute.

## 10. Trust, proof, and demo rules

- Testimonial requires approved display name, role/business context, specific before/after or result, and permission status.
- Logo row uses approved customer logos only. Omit it when the set is too small.
- Case study includes business context, problem/opportunity, Webzoka intervention, and measured/observed result.
- Metrics require a source. Never fabricate percentages or fill empty proof slots with decorative numbers.
- Explicit placeholders are safer than polished fabrication: `[Attributable testimonial: name, role, business]`, `[Approved customer logo]`, `[Case-study metric + source]`.
- Keep one current validated operational proof at a time. Detailed product browsing belongs in Store.
- Renewal terms remain unresolved until a single official commercial source is approved.

## 11. System tokens

| Token family | Direction |
|---|---|
| Container | Wide desktop content rail around 1200–1280px; 16px mobile gutters. |
| Grid | 12-column desktop; 4-column tablet; single-column mobile flow. |
| Spacing | Use `4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 64 / 96px`. |
| Breakpoints | `375 / 768 / 1024 / 1440px`; validate the exact `390px` target. |
| Display type | Editorial serif such as Newsreader or equivalent; hero 64–72px desktop, 42–48px mobile. |
| Body type | Humanist sans such as DM Sans or equivalent; 16px minimum body, 1.55–1.7 line-height. |
| Labels | 12–14px only for metadata/status; readable at 390px. |
| Ink | `#102027` strong; `#42525A` soft body text. |
| Paper/surfaces | `#F6F3EE` paper; `#FFFFFF` surface; `#ECE9E2` muted surface; `#D8D4CB` line. |
| Signal | `#2457D6` action/link/live/focus; `#163C9D` pressed; `#E8EEFF` soft selected state. |
| Semantic | `#C9821B` proof amber; `#147A5E` success/live; `#8A5A00` warning/pending; `#B33D3D` errors only. |
| Radius | 28–32px large; 20–24px standard cards; 14–16px compact; pills only where useful. |
| Depth | Flat/soft default; one featured lift; no glow system, glassmorphism, or dashboard blur. |
| Focus | Visible 2–4px signal-blue focus ring with offset. |
| Motion | 150–300ms micro; transform/opacity only; reduced-motion fallback; no parallax or perpetual marquee. |
| Layering | Content 0; sticky shell 10; overlays 20; mobile CTA 40; modal 100. |
| Icons | One consistent SVG icon family; never emoji as structural UI. |

All normal text/background pairs require at least 4.5:1 contrast. Use color as a supporting signal, not the only indicator of importance or status.

## 12. Mobile 390px contract

- No horizontal page scroll; use 16px side gutters and preserve readable line length.
- Sidebar becomes a drawer. Keep menu, close, links, and primary actions at least `44 × 44px` with adequate separation.
- Order remains: hero copy → evidence → pain/outcomes → Website → Portal → Bundle → signature → portfolio → process → pricing → trust → FAQ → final CTA.
- Proof anchors become readable rows, not tiny inline chips.
- Pain/outcome rows, offer cards, portfolio, process, pricing, testimonials, and FAQ stack vertically.
- Signature connector becomes vertical: Website frame → connector → Stock/system frame.
- Pricing order remains starting price → renewal clarity → calculator handoff.
- FAQ is full-width; no nested horizontal scrollers for essential content.
- Sticky WhatsApp bar, if used, is opaque enough for contrast, reserves bottom space plus safe-area inset, shows one action, and never covers answers or the final CTA.
- Body text stays at least 16px; controls stay at least 44px; inputs, if later added, stay at least 16px to avoid mobile zoom.
- Respect reduced motion; content order and status labels must remain clear without animation.

## 13. Keep, reshape, remove from V6

### Keep

- Left-navigation identity and a unified family across Public, Store, and Hub.
- Store as the buying surface; Hub as the post-purchase surface.
- Portfolio, articles, FAQ, commitments, order flow, product detail, template preview, and maintenance-pricing concepts where they answer a buyer question.
- Unified account concept, with one `Masuk Hub` label.
- The Website → Portal → Bundle buying story and the single signature Website → operations composition.

### Reshape

- Keep the V6 shell, but make the content canvas editorial, richer, more asymmetric, and more spacious.
- Make **Beranda** the default Public page instead of opening directly into Store/catalog.
- Turn the broad catalog into an outcome-led Website / Portal / Bundle decision.
- Move dense browsing, calculator details, templates, and order detail to Store.
- Keep `Berhenti Jadi Admin di Bisnis Kamu Sendiri.` as the post-hero pain statement.
- Replace repeated claims and generic feature tiles with honest status labels, business outcomes, and attributable proof.
- Keep the signature dark section as one macro visual moment.

### Remove or hold back

- Top-navigation-only Public shell — superseded by the latest left-navigation decision.
- Broken LMS, Clinic, Pharmacy, Travel/Rental, and Laundry demo routes until repaired and validated.
- Broken custom portfolio domains, unattributed testimonials, unapproved logos, and unsupported metrics.
- Generic dashboard chrome, purple gradients, neon AI styling, ecosystem diagrams, repeated `bukan mockup` claims, and badge clutter.
- Any section that exists only because a feature is available and does not answer a buyer question.
